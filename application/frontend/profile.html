Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I scan a physical prescription?

Click "Scan Prescription" in the Home Scanner bar, upload or snap a camera photo, and CuraBot AI OCR will extract all medicines into your reminder schedule!

How does generic medicine savings calculation work?

Our Generic Calculator analyzes active chemical salt compositions and compares brand prices with WHO-GMP generic equivalents to calculate 60-80% price savings.

Yes! All records are protected under HIPAA standards using 256-Bit AES encryption. You can export or delete your data at any time in Privacy Settings.
